The Intercept Reality

Adversaries and nation-states are actively harvesting and archiving petabytes of encrypted TLS traffic from fiber taps, undersea cables, and proxy mirrors today.

Because classical ephemeral key exchange (ECDHE / RSA) relies on the discrete logarithm problem, a quantum computer with ~2,330 logical qubits running Shor's algorithm will decrypt every stored session key in minutes. Only X25519MLKEM768 hybrid encryption immunizes data today.
